Travel

From October 2025 to May 2026, I backpacked across Asia, and was taught many things about myself and the world. I believe this journey has shaped me in ways that nothing else could have, and I am incredibly proud of it and grateful for the experiences it provided.

Should change the style here to be a little more fun. Maybe clouds covering each destination until you scroll to it?

Kerala, India

October 2025

Flew into Kochi, Kerala and began travelling as part of a relative's wedding celebrations.

Bangkok, Thailand

November 2025

From India, travelled to Thailand and began the non-family related travels.

Hanoi, Vietnam

December 2025

From Thailand, travelled to Vietnam with the hopes of slowly travelling southwards until we reached Ho Chi Minh City.

Singapore, Singapore

January 2026

Having recouperated from the first leg and seen family, we continued our journey by visitng a relative in Singapore.

Tokyo, Japan

January 2026

Following a short stay in Singapore, we continued our journey to Tokyo, Japan. This would be the first time we visited the country, and we would spend the next 3 months exploring all over Japan.

Seoul, South Korea

April 2026

To conclude our journey, we visited Seoul, South Korea.
